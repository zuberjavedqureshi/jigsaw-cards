# W4-R2 — Four-Image Visual Prototype (specification)

> Status: **proposed, not started** (prepared in W4-R1, 2026-11-28). It starts only after the product owner approves the W4-R1 PRD revision and this specification. It produces review material, not game content: no pictures are registered and no gameplay, progression, difficulty or board code changes.

## 1. Objective

Prove that the approved hybrid direction (PRD VI-01, VI-02, VI-07) produces pictures that are visually exciting, clearly different from each other, legally clean and suitable for 4×4 and 5×5 puzzles, **before** any full location is produced.

## 2. The four subjects

| # | Subject | Style | Dominant colours | Composition | Texture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Colourful fishing harbour: boats, nets and buoys | Realistic photograph | Saturated primaries (red, yellow, teal hulls) on deep harbour blue | High three-quarter view; boats fill the frame; quayside with crates, ropes and nets in front; colourful harbour houses across the top third instead of sky | Painted wood, rope, netting, rippled water |
| P2 | Steam train crossing a turquoise gorge | Semi-realistic illustration | Turquoise water, warm grey stone, pine green, dark green and red locomotive | Diagonal stone viaduct from lower left to upper right; waterfalls on the left; pines on the right; river rocks along the bottom; layered cliffs instead of a sky band | Stone, foliage, mist, water spray, metal |
| P3 | Colourful fruit-market flat-lay | Realistic food photograph | Citrus orange, kiwi green, fig purple, blueberry blue, lime | Top-down (90°) view of wooden crates edge to edge, each filled with a different fruit; cut fruit and leaves vary the pattern | Fruit skin and flesh, wood grain, leaves |
| P4 | Red panda in an autumn maple tree | Semi-realistic illustration | Rust, amber and gold, with green leaves for contrast | Animal about 30% of the frame, slightly off-centre on a diagonal branch; bark in front; misty forest with distinct trunks behind; a small detail in one corner (bird, fungus shelf) | Fur, bark, leaves, soft atmospheric depth (not blur) |

The four differ in dominant colour (blue and primaries, turquoise and stone, citrus mix, rust and gold), composition (three-quarter wide, diagonal landscape, top-down flat-lay, character portrait) and texture (man-made and water, rock and foliage, organic food, fur and leaves).

## 3. Production method and prerequisites

| # | Method | Prerequisites (provided by the product owner) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Licensed stock photograph (or commissioned photograph) | A stock account and a licence tier that permits in-app use in a commercial mobile game; budget approval |
| P2 | AI-assisted illustration with human review and retouching (or a commissioned illustration) | Choice of AI provider and plan; confirmation that the plan's current terms grant commercial rights to outputs; account access usable from this environment, or images generated by the product owner and handed over with their generation records |
| P3 | Licensed stock photograph (or commissioned photograph) | As P1 |
| P4 | AI-assisted illustration (or a commissioned illustration) | As P2 |

**Current blockers (checked 2026-11-28).** This development environment has no image-generation tool: the connected ElevenLabs service is audio-only. There is no stock-photography account or licence, and VI-07 forbids using any image whose rights are not verified. W4-R2 therefore needs the prerequisites above before production can start. If they are not available, the milestone stops and reports what is missing. It **never** substitutes code-drawn or unverified images.

**Rules for AI-assisted work:** prompts describe subject, composition, light and texture only; no artist names, brands, franchises or characters. Outputs are checked for logos, text, watermarks and artefacts. Prompts, model, date and any seeds are saved in the provenance record.

## 4. Deliverables

All deliverables live in the W4 worktree as **review material**, under `artwork/prototype/w4-r2/`. Masters too large for the repository are stored in the owner's storage and referenced.

1. **Four full-resolution images:** `proto-0N-<subject>.webp`, 2048 × 2048 (VI-11), plus the master files (≥ 3000 px).
2. **A contact sheet:** the four pictures numbered and named, each also shown with its **4×4 and 5×5 grid overlay**. Delivered as a viewable link (a private review page), because the product owner reviews on an Android phone.
3. **Provenance records:** `artwork/provenance/proto-0N-<subject>.md`, complete per ARTWORK_GUIDELINES §3, with licence or terms evidence referenced.
4. **A visual quality review:** the 10-point checklist (ARTWORK_GUIDELINES §5) for each picture, with pass or fail and notes.
5. **A puzzle suitability assessment:** for each picture at 4×4 and 5×5:
   - automated tile distinctness (minimum pairwise difference);
   - the weakest tile pairs, marked on the overlay;
   - estimated difficulty;
   - edge and corner tile quality;
   - a recommendation for Normal, Hard or both.
6. **Measurements:** file size per WebP, and decode time and decoded memory for a sample on the test machine (device measurement follows in the engineering milestone).

## 5. Acceptance criteria

- Four pictures exist, one per subject, matching the style and composition in §2, with visibly different colours, compositions, textures and subjects.
- Each picture is 1:1, 2048 px, WebP, at most 800 KB, with no text, logos, watermarks or identifiable people.
- Each picture has a complete provenance record with commercial use **confirmed**. Any "unclear" entry fails the picture.
- Each picture passes the quality checklist or is reported with its failures; the automated tile-distinctness check passes at both 4×4 and 5×5.
- The product owner reviews the contact sheet on their phone and approves or rejects each picture and the overall direction.

## 6. Out of scope

- Raster rendering in the game (the separate engineering milestone; see [RASTER_ARTWORK_PROPOSAL.md](../architecture/RASTER_ARTWORK_PROPOSAL.md)).
- Registering pictures, renaming worlds, or any gameplay, progression, difficulty or board change.
- Producing the remaining levels of any location, and W5.
- Committing or pushing without separate authorisation.

## 7. Estimated effort (once prerequisites exist)

- Stock photographs (P1, P3): about 1–2 hours each for search, licence check, selection and quality review.
- AI-assisted illustrations (P2, P4): about 2–4 hours each for iterations, retouching, artefact checks and records. A commissioned illustration takes days instead.
- Contact sheet, suitability analysis and report: about half a day.
