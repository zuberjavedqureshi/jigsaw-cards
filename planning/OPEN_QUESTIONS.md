| Q-010 | Placement rule: strict (only correct position accepted) or free? Do correct pieces lock? What is the precise incorrect-drop behaviour? | Puzzle engine | Product / Tech | **Resolved 2026-11-23** (product owner): only the correct position accepts a piece; correctly placed pieces are permanently locked; incorrectly dropped pieces animate back to their previous position; locked pieces cannot be moved or swapped. Every tile always occupies exactly one cell of a fixed rectangular grid. The player drags one tile onto another and releases; the two tiles exchange cells. Incorrect swaps are allowed and never rejected; swaps are unlimited and have no penalty. A drag released outside another tile is cancelled and the tile returns to its cell.
